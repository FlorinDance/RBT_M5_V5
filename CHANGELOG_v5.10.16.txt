RBT M5 Hybrid v5.10.16

Live Recovery failure protection

- Default Recovery mode is RECOVERY_ONLY (1).
- The new protection executes real order actions when enabled.
- It starts collecting one floating-profit sample per minute after Recovery
  triggers at -20.00 account-currency units for the 0.50 reference lot.
- At 60 minutes after that trigger, it evaluates the position once, while the
  original Recovery state is still waiting (the position has not reached the
  +10.00 arm threshold).
- It arms the secondary stop only when floating profit is at or below -40.00,
  at least 90% of the samples from the last 30 minutes are negative, and the
  least-squares profit slope over that window is negative.
- The secondary stop is -160.00 for the 0.50 reference lot. All monetary
  thresholds use the existing InpRecoveryScaleWithLot and
  InpRecoveryReferenceLot scaling.
- The stop price is solved with OrderCalcProfit and placed at the broker. If
  price has already crossed the calculated stop, the EA requests an immediate
  position close. Existing tighter stops are never weakened.
- If the position reaches the existing +10.00 Recovery arm threshold before
  the secondary stop is hit, the existing +5.00 protection supersedes it.
- After an EA/terminal restart, the filter takes no action at the checkpoint
  unless it has enough samples to cover the requested window.
